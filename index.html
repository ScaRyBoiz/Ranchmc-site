Life on the ranch

Everything on RanchMC grows out of your farm. Plant, harvest, cook, sell, and spend what you earn making the place bigger.

🏡Your own farm

Claim a plot that's yours alone. Expand it with Tokens as you grow, and invite friends to a co-op to farm it together.

🌽15 crops to unlock

Start with wheat and work up to berries, tomatoes and garlic. Higher-level crops take longer to grow and sell for far more.

🥧Factories

Turn raw crops into jam, juice, pies, cheese and cloth in your own Oven, Jam Maker, Juice Press, Dairy, Loom and Smokehouse.

📋The Order Board

Fill orders for specific goods to earn bonus coins and XP. New orders roll in as you finish them.

🐔Animals and pets

Raise chickens, cows, sheep and pigs in coops, feed them from your Feed Mill, and level up a pet that boosts your farm.

🛒A player economy

Set up shop signs, list goods on the player market, and trade with other ranchers. Climb the farming levels and prestige.
